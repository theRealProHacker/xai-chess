xai-chess

Grading the reasons,
not just the moves.

An intervention-grounded benchmark for chess move-explanations.

Given an explanation of the form “move m is good because of factor X” (say, a pin), xai-chess asks one question: is factor X actually causally responsible for the move being good, or a red herring the explainer name-dropped? It answers by intervention — neutralize the cited factor, then read how much a strong oracle engine’s valuation of the move changes.
