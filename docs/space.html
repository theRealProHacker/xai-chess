Space Visualizer

Every square is shaded by who attacks it more often: pale blue for White, dark brown for Black, hatched where both attack it equally. Arrows show where the structure points — one per pawn chain, drawn from its base out past the tip. Push a pawn or load a structure and both recompute. No engine: control is attack-counting, direction is geometry. This is the site's descriptive demo — space is the one factor the benchmark cannot cleanly switch off, so it gets measured rather than intervened on.

 
 
Square control by attacker count, not an engine evaluation. A square is contested when both sides attack it equally often.
