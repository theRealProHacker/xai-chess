Annotators vs attribution

Does Leela's feature attribution agree with what a master says about a position?

Tint: the concept's attribution on that square, green for the named side, red against. Dashed circle: piece taken off.

How it is scored

Integrated gradients through the layer-0 decoder, 256 steps. A concept's attribution is the sum over its bits, signed for the side named. With / without: the board with the credited piece taken off (or the move taken back); both the sum and its share of all |attribution| must move as expected. Values are signed for the side the test names: positive helps it. Share = the sum over the board's total |attribution|. Descriptive: the sign of the sum; “≈ 0” means at or below the 25th percentile of the same concept in 300 game positions. A game agrees only if every test passes.
