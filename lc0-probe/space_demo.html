Space by piece

Which pieces does Leela credit with space?

Green gains space for its side, red loses it. Kings are not scored.

How it is measured

Integrated gradients of Leela's value through the layer-0 feature decoder, 256 steps, from the average bits of any piece on each square, along the control bits only. A piece's value: its own control bits, each weighted by how far up the board the controlled square is for its side. Kings are left out.

Shared: one path moves every control bit at once, so interactions are split among the pieces. Alone: one path per piece moves only its own control bits; everything else stays as on the board. Z-score: the Alone values standardised to mean 0, variance 1 over all 2,008 scored pieces.
